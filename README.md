<h2 align="center">Engineering the bridge between life, intelligence and machines.</h2>
<p align="center">
  I am a <b>Biomedical Engineering student</b> focused on the intersection of 
  <b>biotechnology</b>, <b>artificial intelligence</b>, <b>robotics</b>, 
  <b>biosensors</b>, and <b>medical device systems</b>.
</p>
<p align="center">
  My goal is to design technologies that are not only functional, but also meaningful:
  systems that understand biological complexity, support healthcare innovation,
  and transform engineering knowledge into real-world impact.
</p>
---
## Vision
```txt
Biology gives the system life.
Electronics gives it perception.
Software gives it intelligence.
Design gives it meaning.

I am interested in building intelligent biomedical technologies that combine hardware, software, biological analysis, and human-centered design.

My long-term direction is centered around AI-supported biomedical innovation, especially in fields such as biosensing, organ-on-a-chip systems, biomedical robotics, and computational healthcare technologies.
```
