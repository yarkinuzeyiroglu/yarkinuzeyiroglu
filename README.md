### 🧬 &nbsp; About Me

Dual-degree student in **Biomedical Engineering & Electrical–Electronics Engineering** at İzmir Bakırçay University.  
Working at the frontier where **machine learning meets biomedical systems** — from deep diagnostic models to biosensor architecture.

- 🔬 &nbsp; Researching **AI-powered medical image analysis** — segmentation, classification, intelligent diagnostics
- ⚡ &nbsp; Dual-discipline foundation: signal processing, embedded systems, and biomedical device design
- 🧫 &nbsp; Fascinated by **organ-on-a-chip** systems, microfluidics, and next-gen in-vitro diagnostics
- 🧠 &nbsp; Tracking how **transformer architectures** and foundation models are reshaping computational medicine
- 🎯 &nbsp; Mission: build AI-guided diagnostic infrastructure that is accessible to everyone, everywhere

> *Engineering tools that bring life-saving precision medicine within reach of everyone.*
