## 👋 About Me

I am **Yarkın Üzeyiroğlu**, a **Biomedical Engineering student** with a strong interest in the intersection of **biotechnology, artificial intelligence, robotics, sensors, and biomedical device systems**.

My main vision is to develop technologies that do not only solve technical problems, but also create meaningful impact in healthcare, research, and human-centered engineering.

I am especially focused on:

- Biomedical systems and medical device design  
- Artificial intelligence in healthcare and biotechnology  
- Biosensors, microfluidics, and lab-on-a-chip systems  
- Robotics, embedded systems, and sensor-based platforms  
- CAD design, simulation, and engineering analysis  
- Interdisciplinary research between biology, electronics, and software
